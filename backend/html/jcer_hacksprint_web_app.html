Agenda

A hackathon is an event where individuals or teams come together to collaboratively work on solving specific problems or challenges within a limited timeframe, usually ranging from a few hours to a couple of days. It is a great platform for programmers, designers, entrepreneurs, and other creative minds to showcase their skills, creativity, and problem-solving abilities.
Vision
To create a dynamic platform that inspires innovation, creativity, and problem-solving among students by fostering a culture of building impactful solutions within a limited time. HackSprint aims to empower young minds to think beyond boundaries and transform ideas into reality.
Mission
The mission of HackSprint is to bring together passionate and curious minds to collaborate, innovate, and build meaningful solutions within an intense 24-hour environment. It aims to promote hands-on learning, encourage teamwork, and provide participants with the opportunity to apply their skills to real-world challenges. Through this initiative, we strive to create a space where ideas turn into impactful prototypes, while fostering a strong culture of innovation and technical excellence at JCER College Belgaum.

FAQs

What is this Hacksprint hackathon about?

It is a National-level hackathon where students build innovative solutions for real-world problems and is organized by Jain College of Engineering and Research under the Computer Science Department

Is there any registration fee?

Yes, a registration fee of ₹1000 per team is applicable. This fee covers event participation and associated arrangements.

What is the team size?

Each team must consist of a minimum of 2 members and a maximum of 4 members.

What is the duration of the hackathon?

The hackathon will run for 24 hours

Can we use pre-built projects?

No, all projects must be developed during the hackathon.

When will the problem statement be released

The problem statement will be released on the website and in the group at 7 a.m. on the day of the hackathon. Teams can then select a problem statement and start working.
